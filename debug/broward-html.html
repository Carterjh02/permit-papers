    Adjusted Building Square Footage

    The adjusted building square-footage is calculated from air-conditioned and non air-conditioned space.

    Building Under Air Square Footage

    The total square footage of attached, livable area excluding balconies, porches, garages, car ports, elevators, and utility rooms.

    Effective Year

    The effective year generally represents the tax year the building value was extended on the tax roll for the first time. It is normally the year after the certificate of occupancy was issued for the building. In some instances, the effective year is adjusted when the property was significantly renovated or remodeled. This information is not a substitute for official county/city building department records nor should it be relied upon for insurance purposes.

    Actual Year

    The actual year built normally represents the year the certificate of occupancy was issued for the building.

    Portability

    Florida's Portability law allows property owners to transfer their “Save Our Homes” benefit earned on a previous Homestead property to their new Homestead property. Click here for more information on portability eligibility and filing instructions.
    Learn More

    Assessed / SOH

    Pursuant to the 1992 "Save Our Homes" (SOH) Amendment to the Florida Constitution, the assessed value of your Homestead property can increase by no more than 3% above last year's assessed value (or the consumer price index, whichever is less). Click here for more information on Save Our Homes & Homestead eligibility and filing instructions.
    Learn More

    Granny Flat

    Taxpayers who build additions onto an existing Homestead or perform extensive renovations to an existing Homestead to provide living quarters for a parent or grandparent may be entitled to a special exemption equal to the amount of the new construction (up to 20% of the homestead value). Click here for more information on Granny Flat eligibility and filing instructions.
    Learn More

    Homestead

    All legal Florida residents are eligible for a Homestead Exemption on their homes, condominiums, co-op apartments, and certain mobile home lots if they qualify. You are entitled to a Homestead Exemption if, as of January 1st, you have made the property your permanent home or the permanent home of a person who is legally or naturally dependent on you. The Florida Constitution provides this tax-saving exemption on the first and third $25,000 of the assessed value of an owner/occupied residence. Click here for more information on Homestead eligibility and filing instructions.
    Learn More

    Add. Homestead

    If you already have a homestead exemption on your property, you don’t have to do anything in order to receive the additional homestead exemption adopted by Florida voters in 2008. The property appraiser will automatically apply the additional exemption of up $25,000 to your assessed value on your homesteaded property. Click here for more information on Homestead eligibility and filing instructions.
    Learn More

    Wid/Vet/Dis

    Widowed: Person who is a bona fide resident of this state is eligible for an exemption of up to $500 of their property’s assessed value. Click here for more information on Widow & Widower eligibility and filing instructions.
    Veterans:This exemption fully exempts any homesteaded veteran with a Total and Permanent Service-Connected Disability rating from paying all ad valorem property taxes each year. Click here for eligibility information and filing instructions about the various veteran and military exemptions available.
    Disability:Full Exemption for certain quadriplegic and low income totally and permanently disabled person.

    Learn More

    Seniors

    Many Florida low income senior citizens are now eligible to claim an additional $25,000 Exemption -- which applies only to the County's portion of the taxes and city taxes for residents of cities which also adopted the additional exemption. By local option, the county and cities may adopt an ordinance increasing this exemption "up to $50,000" and/or the Long-term Residency Senior Exemption. Click here for more information on Senior Exemption eligibility and filing instructions.
